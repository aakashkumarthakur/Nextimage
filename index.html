FAQ

Common Questions

Is my image uploaded to a server?
No. Every conversion happens locally in your browser using the Canvas API — your files never leave your device.
Which formats can I convert between?
You can convert between JPG, PNG and WebP in any direction, and resize the image at the same time.
Will resizing reduce image quality?
Reducing dimensions is lossless in terms of sharpness for the new size. For JPG/WebP you can also control the compression quality with the slider.
Is this tool free to use?
Yes, completely free with no limits on the number of images you convert.
